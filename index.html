HOW TO PLAY

Study the scene before the timer runs out. Exactly one thing changes: a color, a position, a size, a shadow, or how objects relate to each other.

Don't only memorize what you see. Memorize where things sit in relation to one another.

Earn coins for correct answers and spend them on a hint, extra time, a life or a skip. Speed Blink shows the scene for under a second. Some flickers are decoys. Three hearts. A wrong answer costs one. Build a streak for bonus points.
